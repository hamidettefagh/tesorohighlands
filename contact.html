How to reach the neighbors behind this site.

✏️ Spot something wrong?

An out-of-date phone number, a wrong trash day, a broken link. Corrections are the most useful thing you can send, and they usually go live the same day.

Tell a neighbor in the community group chat, or send it straight to the people who maintain the site:

Report a correction ↗

💬 Want to join the community group chat?

That's where alerts get shared, events get planned, and questions get answered by people who live here. We don't post invite links on the public internet, so bots and non-residents can't wander in.

Ask any neighbor you know to add you, or introduce yourself at the pickleball courts.

🛠️ Want to help build the site?

It's open source and built by neighbors. Fix a typo, add a data source, or take on something bigger. Read the contributing notes first; they explain the rules that keep the site honest.
