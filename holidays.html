Top picks

If you only do three things. All are within 35 minutes of us and work for little kids, toddlers included. Put together by a neighbor with little ones.

1 Sat, Nov 21 · 4–8 PM · free · 15 min

Light Up Main Street, Old Town Newhall

Just go. Twenty tons of snow, Santa photos and the tree lighting around 5:30 PM. Parking is free, and so is Santa Clarita Transit on routes 4, 5, 6 and 14.

Griffith Park Holiday Lights Train, then L.A. Zoo Lights

Ride the train at 5 PM, then drive five minutes to the zoo. Book both for the same date: train tickets are $10 each, and Zoo Lights is timed entry.

Christmas light train rides

Book the Griffith Park train. It's a real train looping a mile of light displays, $10 a person, and the 10-minute ride is the right length for a toddler.

Griffith Park Holiday Lights Train Top pick 35 minNov 21 – Jan 3, nightly · $10 a person
Trains about every 20 minutes, 5–8 PM Monday to Thursday and 5–9 PM Friday to Sunday. Closed Nov 26, Dec 24, 25 and 31. Only babies under 18 months ride free. Riders step up 8 inches to board.

Booking: buy online for a specific date. Weekend and December nights usually sell out within 60 to 90 minutes of the 5 PM window opening; weeknights are easier. Priority Entry skips lines that can run 20 to 90 minutes. Parking is free; bring a flashlight. The site lists Nov 21 as opening day but also says "day after Thanksgiving," so confirm when you book. Griffith Park trains ↗
North Pole Railway, Travel Town 35 minNew this year · Nov 27 – Dec 24 · from about $47 adult, $27 child
The train car stays put (screens, fog and motion effects), then you walk a lit village with historic trains, s'mores and Santa. Plan about 90 minutes. Listed at $39.99 adult and $19.99 child before fees; under 3 free. VIP adds a Santa photo and shorter lines. Closed Nov 30, Dec 7 and 14. North Pole Railway ↗
The Polar Express, Perris 1 hr 45 minSelect Nov and Dec dates · $49–$88 a person
The full Santa-on-a-train production: a one-hour onboard show with Santa, cocoa and the book read aloud. About two hours on site; arrive 60 minutes before showtime. Adults $56.25–$88.25, ages 2–11 $49.25–$81.25, under 2 free on a lap. Southern California Railway Museum ↗

Light walks and gardens

L.A. Zoo Lights 35 minNov 20 – Jan 10 · timed tickets from $19 · free parking
An all-new lantern trail of plants, pollinators, marine life and Ice Age giants. A 60 to 90 minute self-guided walk, rain or shine; box office open 5:45–8:45 PM. Under 2 free. Extras: Santa photos on select nights, an ice rink (separate ticket), carousel, s'mores, churros and cocoa.

Closed Nov 26 and 30; Dec 1, 7, 14, 15, 16, 24, 25 and 31; Jan 4–7. Special nights: sensory-inclusive Dec 8, Pride Night Dec 17.

Good to know: the animals are in their night quarters, so seeing them is a separate daytime visit. Best nights to pair with the Griffith Park train: Dec 2, 3, 9 or 10, or Dec 21–23 and 28–30. L.A. Zoo ↗
Enchanted Forest of Light, Descanso Gardens Top pick 35 minNov 22 – Jan 9 · 5:30–10 PM · kids from $19
The best light walk near home: a mile-long trail in its 10th season, with a new laser show and snowfall finale, glowing stained-glass houses and giant koi. Timed entry every 30 minutes until 8:30 PM, advance purchase only. Non-members $29–$52 adult, $19–$37 ages 2–12, depending on the night. Holiday market Dec 2–6. Descanso Gardens ↗
Enchant Los Angeles, Santa Anita Park 50 minNov 20 – Dec 27 · prices on its ticket site
A Christmas light maze, a Santa visit, carolers and a shopping village are included. Skating, the snow slide, carousel, train ride and s'mores are add-ons. Enchant ↗

Call Santa

Call the number yourself before you hand over the phone. These lines change from year to year. Santa's Hotline is the one confirmed working this season.

Santa's Hotline(605) 313-4000Your kid leaves Santa a voicemail, then you get a text with a way to replay it, so you hear exactly what they asked for. Open 24/7. Free; normal carrier rates apply. En español: (605) 313-4001. The reindeer line(803) 672-5287That's (803) MR-CLAUS. The reindeer talk about Santa's Christmas Eve plans, the weather and how the naughty-or-nice list works. Free. This one is from earlier years, so test it first. Where's Santa? NORAD, Christmas Eve only1-877-446-6723That's 1-877-HI-NORAD. Volunteers answer and tell kids where Santa is right now. Last year the phones ran 3 AM to 11 PM Pacific on Dec 24. noradsanta.org tracks him online too.

Skip the "Grinch" phone numbers going around. The most-shared one no longer plays the Grinch, according to Snopes.

The season at a glance

  • Sat, Nov 21Light Up Main Street, 4–8 PM. No booking.
  • Nov 20–25Openings: Zoo Lights (Nov 20), the Griffith Park train and Universal (Nov 21), Descanso (Nov 22). Book Zoo Lights, the train and Descanso.
  • Nov 26–29Magic Mountain Holiday in the Park opens Thanksgiving Day. Hollywood Christmas Parade Sunday, Nov 29.
  • Dec 2 or 3Griffith Park train at 5 PM, then Zoo Lights. Book both.
  • Dec 4–6Descanso (holiday market Dec 2–6; book it) or Magic Mountain.
  • Dec 11–13A Christmas Carol on ice at The Cube, Dec 11–12 (book it). Magic Mountain is now open daily.
  • Dec 18–20The Nutcracker, Dec 19–20 (book it). Watch for the Holiday Light Tour and Barnyard Light Tour dates.
  • Dec 21–23Backup weeknights for the train plus Zoo Lights, or Disneyland on a weekday.
  • Dec 26 – Jan 3Magic Mountain daily. Zoo Lights Dec 26–30 and Jan 1–3. Irvine's Train of Lights Dec 26–30. Descanso runs to Jan 9, Disneyland to Jan 6.

Closed nights to avoid. Griffith Park train: Nov 26, Dec 24, 25, 31. Zoo Lights: Nov 26, 30; Dec 1, 7, 14–16, 24, 25, 31; Jan 4–7.
