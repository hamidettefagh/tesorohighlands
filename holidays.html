Light trains

★ Our pickGriffith Park Holiday Lights Train35 min · Nov 21 – Jan 3 · $10 a person

A real train looping a mile of light displays. The 10-minute ride is the right length for a toddler. Trains run about every 20 minutes, 5–⁠8 PM Monday to Thursday and 5–⁠9 PM Friday to Sunday. Closed Nov 26 and Dec 24, 25 and 31.

Booking: buy online for a specific date. Weekend and December nights usually sell out within 60 to 90 minutes of the 5 PM window opening; weeknights are easier. Priority Entry skips lines that can run 20 to 90 minutes. Only babies under 18 months ride free. Parking is free; bring a flashlight. The site lists Nov 21 as opening day but also says “day after Thanksgiving,” so confirm when you book.

Pair it with Zoo Lights, five minutes away: ride at 5 PM, then walk the zoo. Book both for the same night. Dec 2, 3, 9 and 10 are the easiest; Dec 21–⁠23 and 28–⁠30 also work.

L.A. Zoo Lights35 min · Nov 20 – Jan 10 · from $19

An all-new lantern trail of plants, pollinators, marine life and Ice Age giants: a 60 to 90 minute walk, rain or shine. Timed tickets from $19; under 2 free. Box office open 5:45–⁠8:45 PM, and parking is free. Santa photos on select nights, plus a carousel, s’mores and cocoa; the ice rink is a separate ticket.

Closed Nov 26 and 30; Dec 1, 7, 14, 15, 16, 24, 25 and 31; Jan 4–⁠7. Sensory-inclusive night Dec 8; Pride Night Dec 17.

The animals are in their night quarters, so seeing them is a separate daytime visit.

★ Our pickEnchanted Forest of Light, Descanso Gardens35 min · Nov 22 – Jan 9 · kids from $19

The best light walk near home: a mile-long trail in its 10th season, with a new laser show and snowfall finale, glowing stained-glass houses and giant koi. Open 5:30–⁠10 PM, with timed entry every 30 minutes until 8:30 PM.

Theme parks

★ Our pickMagic Mountain, Holiday in the Park10 min · select dates Nov 26 – Jan 3

Light displays, a tree lighting, Santa’s Cottage, story time with Mrs. Claus, a Looney Tunes show, a craft depot, a Tree Lot Maze and a new “Magic of the Holidays” show. Included with a pass or day ticket; s’mores pits and cookie decorating cost extra. Kids 2 and under are free.

Open Nov 26–⁠29, Dec 4–⁠6, then daily Dec 11 – Jan 3. Those dates come from a ticket seller, so check the park calendar.

No pass? The Black Friday Silver Pass is $79; buy by Nov 30 for a free bring-a-friend ticket, usable through Dec 31.
