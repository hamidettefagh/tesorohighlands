    Trick-or-treat on the hill

    • 🕯️LED lights in pumpkins, not candles. We live in a Very High fire hazard zone, and late October can bring Santa Ana winds. If a Red Flag Warning is up, the banner at the top of this page will say so.
    • 💡Porch light on means treats. Off means not tonight, map or no map.
    • 🚗Drive slowly. Our streets are curvy and steep, and kids dart out between parked cars.
    • 🔦Flashlights or glow sticks help drivers see kids after dark.
    • 🩵A teal pumpkin means non-food treats (stickers, glow sticks, small toys), so kids with food allergies can join in.

    The neighbor code keeps this map off search engines and out of casual view. It's not a vault: add your house only if you're happy for neighbors to see a pumpkin there. Only the phone that added a house can change it, and everything is erased at midnight after Halloween. A pin in the wrong place, or a question? Tell me in the group chat.
