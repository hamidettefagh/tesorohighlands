How this list works

  • Free, with no paid spots. Listings are in alphabetical order within each category.
  • Owners list their own business. Each one is checked before it appears, and it comes down the day its owner asks.
  • No home addresses. A shop or office address is fine; where a neighbor lives is not.
  • Listed, not vetted. This site doesn't rank or endorse anyone. Look up a license and read reviews before you hire.
  • Kept current. Each listing is re-confirmed with its owner once a year, and drops off if it isn't.
