List your business

Own a business and live in Tesoro Highlands?

It's free. Send me these in the community group chat and I'll add you:

  1. Your business name, and what you do in one sentence.
  2. How neighbors reach you: a phone number, website, email or Instagram. Only what you want public.
  3. Your license number, if your work needs one. California requires contractors and real-estate agents to show it in their advertising, and it lets neighbors look you up.
  4. A neighbor offer, if you'd like to make one. Optional.

Running a business from home? HOA and city home-business rules still apply; this page is only a list.

How this list works

  • Free, with no paid spots. Listings are in alphabetical order within each category.
  • Owners list their own business. Nobody is added without asking for it, and a listing comes down the day its owner asks.
  • No home addresses. A shop or office address is fine; where a neighbor lives is not.
  • Listed, not vetted. This site doesn't check, rank or endorse anyone. Look up a license and read reviews before you hire.
  • Kept current. Each listing is re-confirmed with its owner once a year, and drops off if it isn't.
